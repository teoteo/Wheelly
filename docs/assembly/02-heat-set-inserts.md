# Chapter 2 — Heat-set inserts

*The published version of this chapter, with pictures side by side and a pinned table of contents, is in [the guide](02-heat-set-inserts.html).*

Before anything is assembled, the printed parts get their brass inserts. Doing them all in one sitting is not just convenience: some of them can only be reached while the part is bare, and the pivot insert is the clearest case - once the arm hangs on that pivot, no soldering iron will ever reach it again. The clutch&#x27;s insert goes in here too, last.

<table><tr>

<td valign="top"><h3>Step 6 — The box: the M4 insert for the preload screw</h3><ul><li>One more goes into the box, and it is the odd one: an <b>M4</b> insert, <b>Ø6.3 × 8.1 mm</b>, into the outer wall behind the spring seat.</li><li>It goes in <b>horizontally, from inside the box</b>, along the axis of the spring: the iron comes across the compartment from the wheel side, through the small round hole in the inner wall, and pushes the insert out towards the outer wall. Keep the iron level. (If your insert tip is too short to reach - on the reference build the 8 mm one was - use a nail instead, heated with the iron: that is how this insert went in.)</li><li><b>Careful:</b> This is the insert the preload grub screw lives in, and the spring pushes on it with 13.5 N for the life of the machine. It is worth the extra minute of getting it square.</li></ul></td>
</tr></table>

<table><tr>
<td width="55%"><img src="img/02-07-heat-set-inserts.png" alt="The panel: three small inserts for the board" width="520"></td>
<td valign="top"><h3>Step 7 — The panel: three small inserts for the board</h3><ul><li>The board panel takes three inserts on its inside face, on top of the posts the board will sit on.</li><li>Two are <b>M2</b>, <b>Ø3.6 × 4.0 mm</b>, and the one at the head end is <b>M2.5</b>, <b>Ø4.6 × 4.0 mm</b>. They are not interchangeable - fit the <b>M2.5</b> tip for that one.</li><li><b>Careful:</b> These are small. Let the iron do the work and stop as soon as the brass is level with the post: a post pushed too far melts short, and the board then sits on the panel instead of on the posts.</li></ul></td>
</tr></table>

<table><tr>
<td width="55%"><img src="img/02-09-heat-set-inserts.png" alt="The clutch: the M3 insert for its grub screw" width="520"></td>
<td valign="top"><h3>Step 9 — The clutch: the M3 insert for its grub screw</h3><ul><li>Take the printed clutch - off the machine, in your hand - and find the channel in the side of the hub. It runs from the outside straight to the shaft bore.</li><li>Push the <b>Ø5.0 × 3.0 mm</b> <b>M3</b> insert into the channel with the <b>M3</b> tip, brass first.</li><li>Keep the iron in line with the channel. The channel is open all the way to the outside for that reason: the shoulders of the tyre reach Ø46 mm, and an iron coming in at an angle lands on them instead of on the insert.</li><li><b>Careful:</b> The insert stops by itself where the channel narrows into the clearance hole for the grub screw. Do not push it past that: there is no way to get it back out.</li></ul></td>
</tr></table>
