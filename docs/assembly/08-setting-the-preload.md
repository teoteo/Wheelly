# Chapter 8 — Setting the preload

*The published version of this chapter, with pictures side by side and a pinned table of contents, is in [the guide](08-setting-the-preload.html).*

The spring has been in its seat since before the arm went in. Now that the arm and the motor are there for it to push against, the grub screw behind the seat sets how hard the clutch is pressed on the filter disc.

## What this chapter needs

| Qty | Part | |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | M4 grub screw · preload | M4 × 14 headless hex GRUB SCREW (12.1 mm from the back of the spring cup to the mouth of the boss) |

**Tools:** 2.0 mm hex key for the preload grub screw.

<td valign="top"><h3>Step 1 — Set the preload with the grub screw</h3><ul><li>The <b>M4 × 14</b> grub screw goes into the <b>M4</b> insert in the outer wall of the box, behind the spring, and presses on the back of the cup. A 2.0 mm hex key.</li><li>Run it in until you feel the spring start to load, then give it 2.4 mm more: the spring goes from 13.5 to 11.1 mm and pushes with about 13.5 N. Through the arm&#x27;s lever that is roughly 7 N of the clutch on the disc, once the wheel is in. The cup has 2.0 mm of travel either way from there, to add or take off preload.</li><li>Check the arm: it must still swing freely and come back by itself. If it sticks, the trouble is in the pivot, not here - go back to chapter 5.</li><li><b>Careful:</b> You will set this again for real in the last chapter, with the wheel in and the motor turning. What you want now is a spring that is loaded, not a spring that is crushed.</li></ul></td>
